Control virtual apps by showing numbers with your fingers. Every button in the camera view has a number. Hold up that many fingers to press it.

  • 5 fingersOpen the menu
  • 1 – 5Press the button with that number
  • FistGo back
  • 1 fingerWrite in the notepad
Tip: the back camera feels like smart glasses. Hold the phone and reach your hand out in front of it, palm facing the camera.
