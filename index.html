AR Duel

Put a duel board on a real table and battle with virtual decks. Collect cards from real booster packs, build your decks, and play cards onto the table: each monster's artwork rises from its zone as a hologram.

  • CollectOpen 3 free packs every day, or buy real booster packs in the Shop with coins
  • DeckBuild up to 10 decks from the cards you own
  • PlacePick a card from your hand and tap a zone on the table
  • BattleTap your monster, then Attack, then the target. Each finished duel earns coins
  • PassTwo players share one phone and pass it each turn

Card data and images come from the YGOPRODeck card database and are cached on this site. You can also scan real cards, or attach your own 3D model (.glb) to any card name under Scan real cards.

My decks

Keep up to 10 decks. A deck can only use cards you own, up to 3 copies of each, and needs 20–60 cards to duel.

Shop

Real booster packs, with each card at its real rarity in that set. A pack has 9 cards (all-foil sets have 5): mostly Commons, a Rare or Super Rare, and a foil slot that is usually a Super Rare. About 1 in 6 packs has an Ultra Rare, 1 in 12 a Secret Rare, and Starlight Rares are 1 in 576. Free packs reset at midnight.
