Looking for your hand…

How to control

Point
Point with your index finger. The ring follows your fingertip and the button under it lights up
Double-tap
Tap your thumb and index finger together twice, quickly, to press that button. One tap does nothing
Notepad
Pinch and hold to write, open your fingers to lift the pen
