AR Duel

Duel on a real table with real cards in AR. Take on the duelists of Malaysia and win their ace cards, test your decks against the computer, or duel a friend online.

On the board: pick a card from your hand and tap a zone; tap your monster, then Attack. Common cards such as Pot of Greed, Raigeki and Mirror Force work by themselves; other effects you carry out by hand. Card data and images come from the YGOPRODeck database. Scan real cards and 3D models

Story

Beat each duelist to win their ace card and coins, and to meet the next one. Rematches pay a third of the coins, with a 1 in 4 chance of another copy of the ace.

My decks

Keep up to 10 decks. A deck can only use cards you own, up to 3 copies of each, and needs 20–60 cards to duel.

Shop

Real booster packs, with each card at its real rarity in that set. A pack has 9 cards (all-foil sets have 5): mostly Commons, a Rare or Super Rare, and a foil slot that is usually a Super Rare. About 1 in 6 packs has an Ultra Rare, 1 in 12 a Secret Rare, and Starlight Rares are 1 in 576. Free packs reset at midnight.
