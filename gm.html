AR Duel GM

Game master settings. Changes reach players the next time they open the game or the Shop.

Forbidden & Limited list

Forbidden cards can't be put in a deck. Limited: 1 copy per deck. Semi-Limited: 2. Players' decks that break the list can't be used to duel until they are fixed. Characters' decks are not affected.

Booster packs on sale

Untick a pack to take it off sale in the Shop straight away (after you save). Starter decks given to new players are not affected.

Limited pack events

An event pack is sold only between its start and end time, then disappears from the Shop. Switch it off or press End now to stop sales at any time. A pack can be a real set (any set in the database, with its real rarities) or your own list of cards with the rarity you choose for each. Event packs cost coins; daily free packs can't be used on them.
