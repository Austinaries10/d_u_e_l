AR Duel GM

Game master settings. Changes reach players the next time they open the game or the Shop.

Forbidden & Limited list

Forbidden cards can't be put in a deck. Limited: 1 copy per deck. Semi-Limited: 2. Players' decks that break the list can't be used to duel until they are fixed. Characters' decks are not affected.

Packs in the Shop

Only the packs listed here are sold. Give each a price in coins and, if you like, a start and end time (leave them empty to sell it all the time). Daily free packs can be used on these. Limited events have their own tab.

Starter decks in the Shop

Players buy these whole (every card in the deck, saved as a ready-to-duel deck). Switch each one on or off and set its price.

Japan-only packs in the Shop

Packs that only came out in Japan (OCG), in the Shop's "Japan only" tab. Their cards come from Yugipedia's card lists. All are on sale unless you switch one off.

Watch ads for coins

Players tap "Watch an ad" in the Shop and get coins when the ad finishes. Pick the ad network you were approved by and enter its ID; "Test" shows a pretend 5-second ad so you can try it first.

Players

Look up a player to change their coins, free packs, cards and progress, reset their password or suspend the account. Changes are saved straight away (no need for Save changes below); the player's game picks them up the next time it opens or syncs.

Limited pack events

An event pack is sold only between its start and end time, then disappears from the Shop. Switch it off or press End now to stop sales at any time. A pack can be a real set (any set in the database, with its real rarities) or your own list of cards with the rarity you choose for each. Event packs cost coins; daily free packs can't be used on them.
