Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains what information AR Duel (the game at duel.cck8.my and its Android app, together "the game", "we") collects, why, and what you can do about it. AR Duel is a free fan-made card game. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Konami or the owners of Yu-Gi-Oh!.

In short: we keep only what is needed to run your account and your game progress. We do not sell your information. Ads, when they are switched on, are provided by an advertising partner that may use cookies to show and measure ads.

1. Information we collect

WhatWhy
Account details: your duelist name, email address and password (stored only as a secure one-way hash, never in readable form)To create your account, let you log in on any device, confirm your email and reset a forgotten password.
Game progress: your cards, decks, coins, wins against each duelist, rewards, quests, profile picture choice and settingsTo save your game on our server so you can continue from any device.
Login sessions: random login tokens and the time you last playedTo keep you logged in securely.
Diagnostic logs: game events such as duel results, saves, errors, the game version, and the size of the game's data on your deviceTo find and fix bugs (for example, progress not saving). They are tied to your duelist name, kept short (older entries are deleted automatically) and only read by the game's developer.
Online duels: your duelist name, deck and moves in a room you create or joinTo play the duel with the other player. Rooms are deleted after one day.
Ad views: how many reward ads you watched today and whenTo give you coins for watching and to apply the daily limit.
Card photos (only if you use the card scanner)The photo is sent to our server and to Anthropic's Claude AI to read the card's name and details. It is not stored afterwards.
Technical data: IP address, browser type and request times, in our web host's standard server logsTo keep the service running and secure (for example, to limit abuse).

We do not ask for your real name, address, phone number, location or payment details. There are no in-game purchases.

2. Information stored on your device

The game stores your settings and a copy of your progress in your browser's local storage and IndexedDB (for example your language, volume and the card pictures you have seen), and keeps game files in the app cache so it opens quickly and works with a weak connection. You can clear this at any time in your browser or Android settings; your account progress stays safe on our server.

3. Advertising

To keep the game free, players may choose to watch a video ad to earn in-game coins. Ads are only shown when you tap "Watch an ad". When ads are switched on, they are provided by one of these partners:

  • AdinPlay
  • GameDistribution
  • Google (AdSense / H5 Games Ads)

These partners and their vendors may use cookies, device identifiers and your IP address to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure results and, where the law allows and you agree, show ads based on your interests. Their use of this information is covered by their own privacy policies, linked above. Where required (for example in the EU, the UK and some US states), you will be asked for your consent first and can change your choice at any time. You can also opt out of personalised Google ads at adssettings.google.com and of many other networks at youronlinechoices.eu or optout.aboutads.info.

4. Other services we use

  • Web hosting and delivery: our host and Cloudflare deliver the site and keep it secure; they process technical data such as your IP address.
  • Card data and images come from YGOPRODeck and translations from the MyCard project. Our server fetches them, so your device does not share your information with those sites.
  • Fonts and 3D library are loaded from Google Fonts and jsDelivr, which receive your IP address when your browser downloads them.
  • Email (confirmation and password reset messages) is sent from our own mail server.
  • Card scanner: Anthropic (Claude) processes the card photo you send, as described above.

5. How long we keep information

  • Account and game progress: for as long as your account exists.
  • Diagnostic logs: only the most recent entries are kept (older ones are deleted automatically).
  • Online duel rooms: deleted after one day.
  • Server logs: kept by our host for a limited period for security.

6. Your choices and rights

You can change your duelist name, email and password in the game, and log out at any time. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your account and all its data, or ask questions about this policy, by emailing admin@cck8.my from the email address on your account (or tell us your duelist name). We will reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example under Malaysia's PDPA, the EU/UK GDPR or California's CCPA), you may have further rights, such as to object to processing or to complain to your data protection authority.

7. Security

Passwords are stored only as secure hashes, connections use HTTPS, and account files are kept outside the public part of the website. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use elsewhere.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it. In some countries a higher minimum age applies for consent to data processing (up to 16); younger players there need a parent's permission.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced in the game.
